# Business context

Claude uses this file to judge every call. The more specific, the better the feedback.

## What we sell
- Product / service: TuniMobile, a B2B platform for distributing electronic prepaid recharge vouchers in Tunisia. Shops sell Ooredoo, Orange and Tunisie Telecom recharges (1 DT and 5 DT) to their customers from a PDA, a printer, a cash register or the app. It works with or without an existing POS. One account can manage several shops. Shops top up a prepaid balance by cash, bank deposit (versement) or bank transfer (virement). We also sell printers and PDAs, and provide technical support.
- Price range / offers: Prices are the standard market prices, the same as everyone else. The price per voucher depends on the shop's last deposit:
  - Bronze (deposit 1–499 DT): 1.140 / 5.600
  - Silver (500–999): 1.130 / 5.580
  - Gold (1,000–2,499): 1.120 / 5.570
  - Platinum (2,500–4,999): 1.115 / 5.555
  - Diamond (5,000+): 1.110 / 5.550
  Bigger deposit = cheaper vouchers = more profit for the shop. There is no subscription fee. Printers and PDAs are available to buy.
- Main competitors: [fill in the names of the other recharge distributors and wholesalers in your area], the operators' own dealer networks, and traditional scratch cards bought from wholesalers.

## Who we call
- Typical customer (B2B / B2C, sector, role): B2B. Small retailers across Tunisia: superettes and groceries, drugstores, phone and GSM shops, kiosks, libraries and copy shops, cafés, publinets. We speak to the owner or manager. Priority targets are small and medium shops that would deposit roughly 100–1,000 DT at a time (Bronze and Silver tiers), because they are the most profitable for us and the most likely to stay active.
- Main pains we solve:
  - No more running out of scratch cards, and no need to buy and store physical stock.
  - All three operators from one balance and one device.
  - No cash tied up in printed cards, and no risk of lost or stolen stock.
  - Sales tracking in real time, with no manual counting.
  - Easy top-up of the balance (cash, bank deposit, transfer), including through a salesman.
  - Extra footfall: customers who come in for a recharge also buy other products.

## Our ideal call
1. Opening / introduction:
   - Greet the owner by name, in Tunisian Arabic or French as the shop prefers.
   - Introduce yourself and TuniMobile in one sentence: "We let your shop sell Ooredoo, Orange and Tunisie Telecom recharges electronically from one balance, with no card stock to manage."
   - Ask for 2 minutes and confirm you are speaking to the person who decides.

2. Discovery questions we must ask:
   - Do you already sell recharges? Which operators, and how (scratch cards, another platform, operator dealer)?
   - Roughly how much recharge do you sell per day or week?
   - Who supplies you today, and what problems do you have with them (stockouts, price, delays, support)?
   - Do you have a POS, a cash register, a printer or a smartphone in the shop?
   - How do you prefer to pay: cash, bank deposit or transfer?
   - Do you have more than one shop?
   - Where exactly is the shop? (Confirm the real address for the account.)

3. Key value points to pitch:
   - One balance, all three operators, available 7 days a week.
   - No physical stock: no stockouts, no losses, no money frozen in cards.
   - Works with what you already have (phone, POS or cash register), with or without POS.
   - The bigger your deposit, the cheaper your vouchers, so the more you earn on each sale.
   - Fast balance top-up and local support from a salesman or our team.
   - Real-time sales history to track your profit.
   - Free to join, with no monthly subscription.

4. Common objections and our best answers:
   - "I already have a supplier." → Keep them if you like and try us alongside. Many shops use us as a backup so they never run out, and you only pay for what you sell.
   - "Your price is the same as everyone else." → Yes, prices are the market standard. The difference is service: no stockouts, all operators in one place, fast top-up and support. And larger deposits get you a better price.
   - "I don't want to deposit money in advance." → You can start small, from 100 DT. The money stays your balance, and you only use it when you sell.
   - "I don't have a POS or a printer." → You don't need one. It works from a phone, and we can supply a printer or PDA if you want one.
   - "Is my money safe?" → Your balance is recorded in your account, every top-up and sale is tracked, and you can see it at any time.
   - "Bank transfers take time." → Your balance is credited quickly and the transfer is confirmed when it reaches our account. Cash top-ups through a salesman are also possible.
   - "I sell too little for this." → Even small shops benefit: there is no stock to buy up front and no minimum volume.

5. Closing / next step we want (appointment, quote, sale...):
   - Main goal: account created during the call or same day, with the shop's real details (name, owner, phone, exact address, existing equipment).
   - Then: first deposit and first sale within 48 hours, with the salesman or support team helping the first sale.
   - If the owner isn't ready: book a salesman visit with a fixed date and time, and send the sign-up link by SMS or WhatsApp.
   - Never end the call without a concrete next step and date.
